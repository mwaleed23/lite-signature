Email Signature Generator

Fill in your details, preview your signature, then download it and add it to your Zoho email. Leave any contact line blank to hide it.

Tip: clear a field to remove that line from your signature (e.g. leave Phone empty to hide it).

Add it to Zoho Mail

  1. Fill in your details above — the preview updates live.
  2. Click Download signature (saves a PNG), or Copy image.
  3. In Zoho Mail: Settings → Signatures → Add.
  4. Click the image icon in the signature toolbar and upload the PNG (or paste the copied image), then Save.
  5. Set it as your default signature for new emails and replies.
